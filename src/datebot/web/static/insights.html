Insights

Strongest signals

Rows whose like rate differs clearly from your average (95% interval excludes it), at least 8 profiles each. With this many stats a few will be flukes: trust big n, and patterns that repeat across related stats. The app you swiped in is a common confounder, so compare with one app selected.

How to read a row

  1. Percentage and bar = of the rated profiles with that value, the share you liked (e.g. 44% with n=116: you liked 44% of 116 profiles). The bar's end is that same number. The bar's scale is always 0–100% (faint ticks at 25/50/75%), in every card.
  2. Whisker (line with end caps) = the uncertainty around the bar's end: the range your true like rate for this group could plausibly be (95%). The bar end is always inside it. More data makes the whisker shorter around the same spot; it does not push the bar past it. A long whisker means "not enough data yet".
  3. Blue dashed line = your overall like rate across all rated profiles. It is at the same spot in every card.
  4. Colour: green/red only when the whole whisker is on one side of the dashed line (clearly different from your average). Grey = can't tell it apart from your average, so a red-ish bar whose whisker crosses the line stays grey.
  5. n = how many rated profiles are in that row.
